Zyneera

Independent Business Venture

My wife and I came up with this idea over a McDonald's table. I took it from that conversation to a production-ready platform — designing the full product, architecting the backend, and building every frontend screen myself. Zyneera is a B2B event procurement marketplace that connects event planners with vendors, replacing the fragmented mess of email, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets with a single end-to-end workflow.

The Platform

Active
Planners

The primary customer. Create events, source vendors, manage quotes, approve invoices, and confirm work delivery.

Vendors

Suppliers — catering, AV, entertainment, photography. Bid on categories, submit quotes, deliver on-site, and invoice through the platform.
